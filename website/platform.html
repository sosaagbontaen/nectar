The Hive — your whole business, in one place

A fully functioning CRM built for coaches, creators and purpose-driven businesses. Leads, payments, clients, meetings, support, marketing and education — no longer scattered across nine tools and a spreadsheet.

Why It Exists

Most leaders lose their week to the admin of being found

Chasing invoices. Rebuilding the same onboarding email. Wondering which lead went cold and when. The Hive exists so the business side of your calling stops competing with the calling itself.

Hours back every week

Follow-ups, invoicing, scheduling and onboarding run themselves once they're set up properly.

Nothing falls through

Every lead, payment, session and support request is tracked in one record you can actually trust.

One experience for clients

Your people log into one branded home instead of hunting through email for the link you sent in March.

Inside The Hive

Everything the platform already does

Lead Generation

Capture forms, landing pages and pipelines that track every inquiry from first touch to signed client — with automated follow-up so nobody goes cold.

Payment Management

Invoices, one-off payments, subscriptions and payment plans — issued, collected and reconciled without a single awkward reminder text.

Client Portal & Relations

A private, branded home for every client: session notes, files, progress, resources and the full history of your relationship in one thread.

Virtual Meeting Space

Host coaching sessions, group calls and workshops inside your own platform — booked, reminded and recorded without bouncing between apps.

Customer Support

Inbound questions, tickets and conversations handled in one queue, with history attached to the right person every time.

Email Marketing

Segmented lists, sequences and campaigns — nurture, onboarding and re-engagement that keeps running while you're on stage.

Announcements

Broadcast news, launches and updates to your whole audience — or to one segment — from the same place you manage them.

Advertising

Run and track campaigns against real pipeline data, so you can see which spend actually produced clients rather than clicks.

Educational Portal

Host courses, cohorts and training libraries with lessons, progress tracking and certification — your teaching, working while you sleep.

…and more, with new capability added as clients need it.
